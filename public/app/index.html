Linked banks

Bilancio reads your accounts. It cannot move money, and access tokens are encrypted before they are stored.

Bilancio uses Plaid to connect your accounts. Your bank credentials are entered with Plaid and are never seen by Bilancio. By connecting an account you agree to Plaid’s End User Privacy Policy, and to ours — see Privacy and Terms. You can review and revoke every connection you have made through Plaid at Plaid Portal.

Leaving

Deleting your account revokes every bank connection at Plaid and erases your accounts, transactions, categories and rules. Your sign-in is removed too. There is no undo.
