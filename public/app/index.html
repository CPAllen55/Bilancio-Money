What's actually left

Safe-to-spend with a pace line, then money in, money out, kept and your savings rate — each against the comparable period before, and each against what the same period was expected to cost.

A year, against the year before

Where the money went each month, stacked by category, with a dashed line showing what that same month cost you a year earlier. Switch on the budget to carry the rest of the year at what you have planned.

What you actually kept

Money in minus money out, month by month, with a running total across the period shown. A bad month inside a good year is not the same thing as a bad year.

Against the plan

How much of each budget has gone. A full bar is the whole budget; past that the bar turns red and a white line marks where the budget ran out, so the red beyond it is the overspend. Pick a month, and how many months back from it to include.

How the budget is worked out

Pick a method per category. A parent's choice applies to everything inside it until a child says otherwise. Each row shows what its method produces for this month, so the choice is made by looking rather than guessing.

Linked banks

Bilancio reads your accounts. It cannot move money, and access tokens are encrypted before they are stored.

Bilancio uses Plaid to connect your accounts. Your bank credentials are entered with Plaid and are never seen by Bilancio. By connecting an account you agree to Plaid’s End User Privacy Policy, and to ours — see Privacy and Terms. You can review and revoke every connection you have made through Plaid at Plaid Portal.

Leaving

Deleting your account revokes every bank connection at Plaid and erases your accounts, transactions, categories and rules. Your sign-in is removed too. There is no undo.
