The dashboards

Seven views, each with a job.

Every screen below is the product as it stands, running on a demonstration account of invented transactions. The headings are the app's own.

Overview

Where things stand

This month against the plan and against the month before it — what came in, what went out, what is left, and the rate you are keeping it at.

Tracker

Against the plan

How much of each budget has gone. Green is within it, red is past it, and a line marks where it ran out — so the overspend is the part beyond the line.

Trend

A year, against the year before

Monthly spend stacked by category, with a dashed line for the same month a year earlier and this year, last year and the difference under every column.

Net Activity

What you actually kept

Money in minus money out, month by month, with a running total across the period. A bad month inside a good year is not the same thing as a bad year.

Budgeting

Your plan

Every category by month, as blocks you can take hold of. Drag one and the figure at the top moves with it, so the cost of a decision is visible while you are still making it.

Net Worth

What you are worth

What you own against what you owe, reconstructed month by month — so the line behind the figure is as real as the figure.

Transactions

Every line

Amounts read the way you think about them: money in is positive. Refile anything, and make a rule from it so the next one files itself.

How it works

How the budget is built

One method, not a menu. It answers what is normal, where it is heading and what shape the year has — all at once, from your own history.

  1. 01

    Find what is normal

    The middle month rather than the average, so one bad month cannot drag the whole figure along with it.

  2. 02

    Set the one-offs aside

    A one-thousand-dollar vet bill is not a monthly pet budget. It comes out of the monthly figure and is provided for separately.

  3. 03

    Keep the shape of the year

    If May is heavy every year because that is when you travel, May stays heavy. The year is not smeared flat.

  4. 04

    Then you move it

    Every figure is a starting point. Drag a month, pin a holiday that is already booked, and watch what it does to the year.

Your data

What we do, and what we will not do.

01

Read-only, always

Bilancio reads your balances and transactions. It has no ability to move money, make a payment, or change anything at your bank — not switched off, not built.

02

Credentials never touch us

You sign in to your bank inside Plaid's own window. Your details are never sent to, seen by, or stored by Bilancio. We hold a token you can revoke, encrypted, and nothing else.

03

Not for sale, not to anyone

No advertising, no analytics, no tracking. Disconnect a bank and its history is deleted rather than archived — the accounts and every transaction under them go with it.

Dark mode

The same product, after dark.

Both themes are drawn against measured contrast rather than eyeballed, so a figure is as readable at midnight as it is at noon. It follows your system, and you can override it.
