Deleting your Bilancio Money account

Bilancio Money is operated by Guardiano del Faro LLC. You can delete your account, and everything in it, from inside the app. Nobody has to ask us, and we cannot undo it afterwards.

In the Android app

  1. Open Bilancio Money and sign in.
  2. Tap More at the bottom right.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE when asked, and confirm.

In the iPhone app

  1. Open Bilancio and sign in.
  2. Tap More, then Banks.
  3. Choose Delete account at the bottom, and confirm.

On the website

  1. Sign in at bilanciomoney.com/app.
  2. Open Banks.
  3. Choose Delete account at the bottom, and confirm.

What is deleted

Everything, immediately:

  • Every bank connection, revoked at Plaid, so no further data can be read.
  • Your accounts, balances and transactions.
  • Your budgets, categories, merchant rules and split transactions.
  • Your notification settings and any device registered for alerts.
  • Your email address and your sign-in.

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing of your financial data is kept. Two things outlive the account, and neither identifies what you spent:

  • Payment records. Stripe, Apple or Google hold the record of a subscription you paid for, under their own terms, because tax law requires it of them.
  • Server logs. Our host keeps request logs for a short period as part of running the service. They are not linked to your account once it is gone.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account cancels a subscription bought on the website or through Google Play. A subscription bought in the iPhone app can only be cancelled by you, in your Apple account under Settings, your name, then Subscriptions — do that first.

Removing one bank instead

If you want to disconnect a single bank but keep your account, open Banks and remove that connection. Its accounts and transactions are deleted with it; your categories, rules and the rest of your account stay.

If you cannot sign in

Email privacy@bilanciomoney.com from the address on your account and we will delete it for you within 30 days.
